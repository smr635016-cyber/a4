Our Story

Made by two people who loved watches and the sky

We're an independent workshop, not a large manufacturer. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A rooftop, a telescope, and a broken watch

Stellar Hour Cove started when a watch repair on a rooftop turned into a much longer conversation about why so few affordable watches were finished with any real care. We started sourcing movements and testing case designs ourselves, in small batches, checking every piece by hand before it left the workbench.

The name comes from those early nights — watching the sky, taking watches apart, and slowly getting better at both.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every piece

Tested before assembly

Every movement is regulated and checked before it goes into a case.

Small batches only

We limit run sizes so every dial gets real hand inspection.

No fixed collection forever

Once a batch sells out, it's retired rather than mass-reproduced.
