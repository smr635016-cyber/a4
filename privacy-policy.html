Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Stellar Hour Cove ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information gathered through this website and direct contact with our boutique. We are a small, independently operated workshop, and this policy applies only to this site and our own communications with customers.

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide — name, email, and your message. We do not require an account or payment details through this website, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Like most websites, our analytics tools may log basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used only in aggregate.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry about a purchase, sizing, or service question. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Analytics and the Google Tag

This site uses Google's tagging service (gtag.js) to understand overall site traffic. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can control this through your browser settings.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain reasonable business records. You may request removal of your information using the contact details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent revision.
